## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a license to hire a handyman in St. Paul, MN?**
A: In Minnesota, a handyman does not need a general contractor license for jobs under $15,000. However, they must still carry liability insurance and workers' compensation. For electrical, plumbing, or HVAC work, a licensed specialist is often required by law, even for small tasks, depending on the complexity. Always verify the specific licensing requirements for your project.

**Q: How much does it cost to hire a handyman in St. Paul?**
A: The average hourly rate ranges from $75 to $100. Many companies charge a service call fee of $60 to $100, which is often applied to the total bill if you hire them. Large projects may be quoted at a flat rate to provide cost certainty.

**Q: Can a handyman fix plumbing or electrical issues?**
A: Handymen can handle minor repairs, such as fixing a running toilet, replacing a faucet, or swapping a light fixture. However, major plumbing or electrical work, including rerouting pipes or updating wiring, must be performed by a licensed professional to ensure safety and code compliance.

**Q: How quickly can a handyman respond to an emergency in St. Paul?**
A: Response times vary by company, but many local St. Paul handymen offer same-day service for urgent issues. For true emergencies like burst pipes or heating failures, some services guarantee arrival within 1-2 hours.

**Q: Are handymen in St. Paul familiar with historic home regulations?**
A: Yes, the top-rated local handymen are well-versed in the St. Paul Historic Preservation Ordinance. They understand the restrictions on exterior changes, window replacements, and siding in designated historic districts, ensuring your repairs are compliant with city codes.
