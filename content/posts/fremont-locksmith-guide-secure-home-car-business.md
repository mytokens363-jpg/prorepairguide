## FAQ

**Q: What is the average cost for an emergency lockout in Fremont?**
A: The average cost ranges from $80 to $150 for residential lockouts and $90 to $150 for vehicle lockouts. Prices may be higher late at night, on holidays, or for high-security locks.

**Q: Can a locksmith program keys for a Tesla or other modern EVs in Fremont?**
A: Yes, top-rated local locksmiths in Fremont possess the specialized equipment required to program transponder keys and key fobs for modern electric vehicles, often at a fraction of the dealership cost.

**Q: How long does it usually take for a locksmith to arrive in Fremont?**
A: Most local emergency services aim to arrive within 15 to 20 minutes, depending on traffic conditions on major arteries like I-880 and Highway 84.

**Q: Do I need a locksmith license in California?**
A: While California does not have a specific state-level locksmith license, many reputable local locksmiths are registered with the Alameda County Sheriff's Office and hold certifications from organizations like ALOA. Always ask for proof of registration.

**Q: What should I do if a locksmith asks for more money than quoted?**
A: If a technician arrives and demands significantly more money than the initial phone quote, do not pay. This is a common scam. Hang up, leave if necessary for safety, and contact a different, verified local provider.

**Q: How often should I re-key my locks in Fremont?**
A: It is recommended to re-key your home locks immediately after moving in, after a lost key, or every 7 to 10 years as locks wear out. Commercial properties should re-key after any employee termination or security breach.
